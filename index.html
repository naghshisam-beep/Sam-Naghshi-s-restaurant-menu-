Our traditional Iranian menu includes a variety of delicious local dishes, such as Gheimeh, Ghormeh Sabzi, Fesenjan, and Baghali Ghatogh. These authentic Persian dishes are prepared with fresh ingredients and traditional recipes, bringing the rich flavors of Iranian cuisine to your table.
Gheimeh

Gheimeh is a delicious traditional Iranian stew made with tender beef or lamb, yellow split peas, onions, tomato paste, and dried lime. It is usually served with crispy fried potatoes and Persian rice.

300,000t

Garden Sabzi

Ghormeh Sabzi is one of the most popular Iranian stews. It is prepared with fresh herbs, including parsley, cilantro, and fenugreek, along with beef or lamb, kidney beans, onions, and dried lime. It is usually served with Persian rice.

250,000t

Baghali Ghatogh

Baghali Ghatogh is a traditional dish from northern Iran, especially Gilan. It is made with broad beans, fresh dill, garlic, eggs, turmeric, and spices. It is a delicious local dish, usually served with rice.

400,000t

Fesenjan

Fesenjan is a rich and flavorful Persian stew made with ground walnuts, pomegranate molasses, onions, and chicken or duck. It has a unique sweet and sour taste and is usually served with Persian rice.
